Prototype notes

  • Sample content. Listings are sample data. Condition, availability and safety aren’t verified.
  • Illustrative geography. The map is a static image of the Twin Cities metro. The purple circle is the 25-mile search area at true scale; listing locations are approximate neighbourhoods. Home zooms out (to 60% at most) only to fit the circle.
  • Results sheet. Drag the handle or sheet header, tap the handle, or use Expand map / Collapse map to switch between list and map.
  • Unavailable. Favorites, Post, Account and Chat, plus Save search, Saved searches, Contact seller, Open in Maps, sort, voice, and the location and distance pickers.
