They replace earlier polished, ad-style images. Each one is exported at 960 × 644 px as JPEG.

Prompt direction: an ordinary phone photo for a classified listing; private sellers in driveways, apartment lots or on residential streets; dealers on a plain independent lot. Natural, often overcast light, normal wear for the stated mileage, and unreadable plates. No people, no dealer branding, no readable text, no skylines or scenic backdrops.
